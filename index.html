HOW TO PLAY PREDICHESS

1. Standard Moves with a Twist

Predichess follows traditional chess rules: castling, en passant, pawn promotion, and standard movement patterns are identical.

2. The Prediction Phase

Immediately after you make your move, you secretly predict which piece and destination square your opponent will choose next (by clicking and dragging one of their pieces).

3. Springing the Trap

If your opponent plays the exact piece and square you predicted, the trap is sprung!

  • The moving piece is instantly vaporized/destroyed.
  • Your opponent loses that piece and must make another move.
  • If you successfully predict their King's move, their King is destroyed and you win the game immediately!

4. Prediction Miss

If your opponent plays any move other than your secret prediction, their move resolves normally, and then they predict your next response.
